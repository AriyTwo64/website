Texas Hold'em Official Guide

Comprehensive rules, betting rounds, actions, and 100-chip hosting tips.

2-10 Players

1. Objective & Basic Setup

In Texas Hold'em, each player is dealt two private cards face down ("hole cards"). Over four betting rounds, five community cards are dealt face up on the board. The goal is to make the best 5-card poker hand using any combination of your 2 hole cards and 5 community cards.

The Dealer Button

Rotates clockwise after every hand to determine position. The player with the button acts last on every betting round except pre-flop.

Small & Big Blinds

Forced bets posted before cards are dealt. Small Blind (SB) is seated directly left of the dealer; Big Blind (BB) sits to the SB's left.

2. The 4 Betting Rounds

ROUND 1: PRE-FLOP 2 Hole Cards

Players receive 2 hole cards face down. Action begins with the player to the left of the Big Blind ("Under the Gun"). Players can Fold, Call the Big Blind, or Raise.

ROUND 2: THE FLOP 3 Community Cards

The dealer burns 1 card and deals 3 community cards face up on the table. Betting starts with the first active player to the left of the Dealer button.

ROUND 3: THE TURN 4th Community Card

A 4th community card is dealt face up. A 3rd round of betting takes place following the exact same order as the flop.

ROUND 4: THE RIVER & SHOWDOWN 5th Card & Reveal

The 5th and final community card is dealt face up. After the final betting round, all remaining players reveal hands. Highest 5-card hand wins the pot.

3. Standard Player Actions

FOLD Surrender cards and forfeit pot
CHECK Pass action if no bet placed
CALL Match the current active bet
BET Place first bet in a round
RAISE Increase existing bet size

Tips for Hosting a Home Game with 360 Chips

  • Optimal Player Count: 360 chips comfortably support full-table games of 6 to 10 players with deep starting stacks (35–50 chips each).
  • Small Chip Balance: With 100x $1 chips, each player can comfortably start with 10–15 single-dollar chips for smooth small-blind posting without immediate change-making.
  • Re-buy Reserve: The 70x $100 and $25 chips offer over $8,000 in total reserve bank depth, providing ample room for cash game re-buys or high-level tournament re-entries.
What is Re-buying?

Re-buying occurs when a player loses all their chips (or falls below a threshold) and purchases an additional stack from the dealer bank to stay in the game.

  • Cash Games: Re-buys are usually unlimited. Give the player larger denomination chips ($25 / $100) from the bank reserve to save small chips on the table.
  • Tournaments: Re-buys are only allowed during specified early levels (e.g., levels 1–4). Afterwards, elimination is final.
What is Coloring Up?

Coloring Up (or "Chip Race") is exchanging many low-value chips for fewer higher-value chips of equal total value (e.g., trading five $1 chips for one $5 chip).

  • Reduces Table Clutter: Keeps chip stacks manageable so games move faster.
  • Tournament Phase Out: When blind levels increase and $1 or $5 chips are no longer needed, trade them into the bank for $25 and $100 chips.

Blackjack (21) Complete Guide

Rules, card values, dealer strictness, player options & payouts.

Player vs Dealer

Game Objective

Beat the Dealer's hand total without exceeding 21. Exceeding 21 is a "Bust" and results in an immediate loss. You are playing strictly against the dealer's hand, not other players.

Card Values

2 – 10 Face Value
J / Q / K Worth 10
ACE 1 or 11

Gameplay Sequence

STEP 1 Place Bets

Each player places an initial wager in chips in front of them.

STEP 2 The Deal

Players get 2 cards face up. Dealer gets 1 face up (upcard) & 1 face down (hole card).

STEP 3 Player Decisions

Players act one by one until they Stand or Bust.

Player Decisions

HIT

Request another card from dealer. Repeat until standing or busting.

STAND

Keep current hand total and end your turn.

DOUBLE DOWN

Double your bet, receive exactly one additional card, then stand.

SPLIT

If dealt a pair (e.g. 8-8), double bet to split into two separate hands.

Mandatory Dealer Rules

Unlike players, dealers have zero choices:

  • Must HIT if hand total is 16 or less.
  • Must STAND on 17 or higher (Soft 17 rules vary by casino).
